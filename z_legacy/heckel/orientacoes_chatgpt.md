### 2. Approach for Insight Analysis

#### Relevance Categorization and Processing

##### High-Relevance Insights
*Directly related to the CPG sector with immediate application potential*

**Full Analysis:**
- **slug**: Short and memorable title
- **source**: Podcast, article, or video and timestamp if applicable
- **core_idea**: Thesis applicable to the context
- **potential_product**: Potential product derived from the idea
- **triggers**: Questions that activate the insight
- **detected_trend**: Related behavioral or technological trend
- **mvp_suggestion**: Simple experiment for validation
- **keywords**: Up to 6 terms for categorization
- **value_comment**: Justification of current relevance
- **open_uncertainty**: Aspects to be discovered or tested

##### Medium-Relevance Insights
*Potentially useful or tangentially related to the context*

**Simplified Analysis:**
- **slug**: Short and memorable title
- **source**: Origin of the insight
- **core_idea**: Main summarized concept
- **potential_product**: Potential application
- **detected_trend**: Related trend
- **keywords**: Terms for categorization

##### Low-Relevance Insights
*Interesting but less applicable to the immediate context*

**Processing:**
- Brief mention without detailed analysis
- Possible complete filtering to maintain focus on the most valuable insights

#### Relevance Evaluation Criteria

- **High**: Direct application for CPG entrepreneurs; alignment with automation, predictive analysis, or dashboard objectives; potential for immediate implementation

- **Medium**: Indirect application; potential for adaptation to the CPG context; medium-term implementation horizon

- **Low**: Tangential application; requires significant adaptation; long-term implementation horizon

## 📝 Guidelines for generating HTML content optimized for Shopify

**Objective:**  
Generate **clean, clear, uniform, and faithful content to knowledge bases**, ready for direct publication on the Shopify blog **without requiring additional review**.

The content should be delivered in **technical HTML format optimized for Shopify**, following these guidelines:

---

### ✅ Structure and markup

1. **Use exclusively** semantic tags such as:
   - `<h2>`, `<h3>`, `<p>`, `<ul>`, `<ol>`, `<li>`, `<strong>`, `<em>`.
   - ❗ **Do not use `<h1>`**, as Shopify **automatically generates** the `<h1>` based on the post's "Title" field.

2. Maintain the **correct hierarchy**:  
   - `<h2>` for main content sections.  
   - `<h3>` or `<h4>` for subtopics, if necessary.

3. Do not include any visual elements, such as:  
   - Inline styles (`style=""`)  
   - Colors  
   - Custom fonts  
   - Specific sizes

4. Do not insert global structure elements, such as:  
   - `<!DOCTYPE html>`  
   - `<html>`  
   - `<head>`  
   - `<meta>`  
   - `<title>`  
   - `<body>`

These elements are **automatically generated by Shopify** and **should not be duplicated**.

---

### ✅ Content and SEO

5. The content should:
   - Start with a **clear introduction**.
   - Be divided into well-defined sections, facilitating reading and scannability.
   - Use **relevant keywords** for SEO naturally.

6. Whenever possible, include **clear and objective calls to action (CTAs)** at the end.

7. ❗ **Do not insert** `<meta name="description">` or `<meta name="keywords">`.  
These information will be configured **directly in the Shopify panel**, in the "Search engine listing preview" section.

---

### ✅ HTML Summary (Excerpt / Summary HTML)

Generate a **short and persuasive HTML Summary** for the "Summary HTML" (Excerpt) field of the post in Shopify.

#### Content optimization
- Keep the summary concise and attractive — **150 to 160 characters** to avoid truncation in search results.
- Include **relevant keywords** naturally in the text.
- Write an **original** summary that doesn't just repeat the post title.
- Focus on the **main benefit** or value proposition the reader will gain.

#### Engagement tactics
- Create summaries that **arouse curiosity** or ask **intriguing questions**.
- Highlight **specific benefits, solutions, or results** that the post delivers.
- Use **action-oriented language** that encourages clicks.
- Include **numbers or specific details** when it makes sense (e.g., "5 proven strategies").

#### SEO considerations
- Incorporate **primary and secondary keywords** naturally, without keyword stuffing.
- Ensure each summary is **unique** among all posts.
- Write **descriptive and faithful** content to the full post.
- Consider including the **brand name** or store focus when relevant.

#### Technical best practices
- When the theme/visual theme supports it, use **basic HTML formatting** to apply emphasis.
- Avoid excessive markup; maintain **readability** and **compatibility** with the theme.

> Example (up to 160 characters):
> "Discover 5 practical strategies to protect hair color in daily life, with usage guidelines and key ingredients for visible results."

---

### ✅ Technical SEO Additions

8. **Reading time indicator**  
Add a short estimated reading time at the beginning of posts to improve engagement, based on a rough estimate of time spent in reading the article
```html
<p><em>⏱️ Reading time: 5 minutes</em></p>
```

9. **FAQ sections for snippets**  
Include FAQ sections when relevant. Use semantic heading hierarchy for better SEO and featured snippet opportunities:  
```html
<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3>How to use GE Beauty boosters?</h3>
<p>Apply the booster after washing, mixing with your favorite leave-in...</p>
```

10. **Table of contents**  
For longer posts, add a table of contents near the start to improve navigation:  
```html
<h2>In this article:</h2>
<ul>
  <li><a href="#benefits">Main benefits</a></li>
  <li><a href="#application">How to apply</a></li>
  <li><a href="#results">Expected results</a></li>
</ul>
```

11. **Internal linking strategy**  
Where relevant, link to related products, guides, or other blog posts. Use only clean anchor links without extra attributes:  
```html
<a href="/products/antioxidant-booster">Discover the Antioxidant Booster</a>
```

12. **Image optimization (when images are included)**  
- Always include `alt` attributes with descriptive text.  
- Use concise descriptions that incorporate relevant keywords naturally.  
- Example:  
```html
<img src="/images/antioxidant-booster.jpg" alt="GE Beauty Antioxidant Booster for color protection">
```

---

### ✅ Brand Voice and Local SEO Considerations

13. **Consistency with brand voice**  
Ensure all content maintains the brand's tone: **inspirational, authentic, and accessible**. Avoid overly technical jargon unless it adds clear value.

14. **English language SEO best practices**  
- Prioritize natural use of English keywords.  
- Include regional terms relevant to the target market.  
- Consider cultural nuances in beauty and lifestyle content.

15. **Local search optimization**  
When relevant, incorporate market-specific terms and context that connects with the local audience.

---

### ✅ Emotional and Engagement Triggers

16. **Emotional hooks in summaries and CTAs**  
- Use curiosity, urgency, or benefits-driven phrasing.  
- Examples:  
  - "Discover how to prevent invisible damage to your hair."  
  - "Want to know the secret to stronger hair in 7 days?"  

17. **Numbers and specifics**  
Where possible, include numbers or concrete results to improve click-through rates and readability.

---

### ✅ Other important points

18. Avoid links that are not essential.  
If inserting, use the structure:  
`<a href="URL">Text</a>`  
Without extra `target` or `rel`.

19. Use **lists** (`<ul>`, `<ol>`) to highlight important points when necessary.

20. **Do not include scripts, iframes, or interactive elements**.

---

### ✅ Emphasis formatting

21. Use `<strong>` to highlight important terms or key concepts.

22. Use `<em>` to mark light emphasis or specific expressions.

---

### ✅ Examples of allowed tags

```html
<h2>How to use GE Beauty boosters</h2>
<p>Boosters offer personalization and intensive treatment for different hair types.</p>

<strong>Main benefits:</strong>
<ul>
  <li>Deep hydration</li>
  <li>Hair strengthening</li>
  <li>Natural shine</li>
</ul>

<h2>Application tips</h2>
<p>For best results, follow the guidelines below:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Choose the ideal booster for your hair type.</li>
  <li>Mix according to instructions.</li>
  <li>Use regularly for lasting results.</li>
</ol>
```

### ✅ Final requirements

- The generated content must be **ready to copy and paste directly** into the Shopify blog post editor.
- Structurally **compatible with SEO best practices** and the platform's publishing logic.
- **Without requiring additional manual adjustments**.

---

**Note:**  
The `<h1>` will be automatically generated from the post's **"Title"** field in Shopify.  
The meta description will be configured in the **"Search engine listing preview"** section.

---

_Pay attention to clarity, objectivity, and hierarchical structure. Avoid redundancies and excessive markup._
